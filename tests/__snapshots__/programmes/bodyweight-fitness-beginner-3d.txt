# 8-week general fitness — 3 sessions/week, 8 weeks
Profile: general-fitness, beginner, 3 days, 45 min
Progression: +2 kg upper / +4 kg lower

Phases:
  W1-3 Re-adaptation: RIR 3 (all-sets)
  W4-5 Volume: +1 set on main blocks 1, 2; cardio +5 min
  W6-7 Intensity: RIR 2 (first-set); cardio +10 min
  W8 Deload + test: volume × 0.6; RIR 3 (all-sets); max test: Push-up

## Session A — Full body A  [a-full-a, ~39 min]
Warm-up:
  1. Arm circles · 30s
  2. Bodyweight squat · 15
  3. 2 × Prone Y raise · 15  (rest 30s)
Main:
  1. 2 × Bodyweight squat · 10-15 · RIR 3  (rest 75s)
  2. 2 × Push-up · 10-12 · RIR 3 · bodyweight  (rest 75s)
  3. 2 × Inverted row under a table · 12 · RIR 3  (rest 75s)
  4. 2 × Good morning · 12 · RIR 3 · tempo 3-0-2  (rest 75s)
  5. 2 × Door towel row · 10-15 · RIR 3  (rest 75s)
  6. 2 × Hands-elevated pike push-up · 12-15 · RIR 3  (rest 60s)
  7. superset × 2 rounds (rest 45s after each round)
       - Plank · 45-60s
       - Reverse crunch · 10-12 · RIR 3
  8. 2 × Step-up · 15/side · RIR 3 · bodyweight  (rest 45s)
Finisher:
  1. Step jacks · 10 × (40s on / 20s off)
