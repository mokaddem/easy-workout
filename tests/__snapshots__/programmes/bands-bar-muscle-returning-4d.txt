# 8-week muscle gain — 4 sessions/week, 8 weeks
Profile: muscle, returning, 4 days, 60 min, 82 kg
Progression: +2 kg upper / +4 kg lower

Phases:
  W1-3 Re-adaptation: RIR 3 (all-sets)
  W4-5 Volume: +1 set on main blocks 1, 2
  W6-7 Intensity: RIR 2 (first-set)
  W8 Deload + test: volume × 0.6; RIR 3 (all-sets); max test: Push-up

## Session A — Upper body A  [a-upper-a, ~46 min]
Warm-up:
  1. Arm circles · 30s
  2. Push-up · 15 · tempo 2-0-2 · bodyweight
  3. 2 × Band pull-apart · 20 · light band  (rest 30s)
Main:
  1. 3 × Push-up · 8-10 · RIR 3 · bodyweight  (rest 90s)
  2. 3 × Inverted row under a table · 10 · RIR 3  (rest 90s)
  3. 2 × Band overhead press · 10-12 · RIR 3 · light band  (rest 75s)
  4. 4 × Chin-up · 6-10 · RIR 3 · bodyweight  (rest 105s)
  5. superset × 4 rounds (rest 60s after each round)
       - Band lateral raise · 15 · RIR 3 · light band
       - Band biceps curl · 10-12 · RIR 3 · light band
  6. superset × 4 rounds (rest 60s after each round)
       - Band overhead triceps extension · 12 · RIR 3 · light band
       - Band face pull · 15-20 · RIR 3 · light band

## Session B — Lower body A  [b-lower-a, ~46 min]
Warm-up:
  1. Bodyweight squat · 20
  2. Walking lunge · 10/side · bodyweight
  3. Glute bridge · 15
Main:
  1. 3 × Box pistol squat · 8-12/side · RIR 3  (rest 90s)
  2. 3 × Single-leg Romanian deadlift · 10/side · RIR 3 · tempo 3-0-2 · bodyweight  (rest 90s)
  3. 2 × Step-up · 10/side · RIR 3 · bodyweight  (rest 90s)
  4. 2 × Single-leg calf raise · 15-20/side · RIR 3 · bodyweight  (rest 45s)
  5. superset × 4 rounds (rest 60s after each round)
       - Plank · 45-60s
       - Hanging knee raise · 10-12 · RIR 3
  6. 2 × Band hip abduction · 20/side · RIR 3 · light band  (rest 45s)

## Session C — Upper body B  [c-upper-b, ~43 min]
Warm-up:
  1. Arm circles · 30s
  2. 2 × Dead hang · 20s  (rest 30s)
  3. 2 × Band face pull · 20 · light band  (rest 30s)
Main:
  1. 3 × Chin-up · 5-8 · RIR 3 · bodyweight  (rest 150s)
  2. 3 × Band overhead press · 10-15 · RIR 3 · light band  (rest 120s)
  3. 2 × Feet-elevated inverted row · 12 · RIR 3  (rest 75s)
  4. 4 × Push-up · 8-12 · RIR 3 · bodyweight  (rest 90s)
  5. superset × 4 rounds (rest 60s after each round)
       - Band biceps curl · 12 · RIR 3 · light band
       - Band overhead triceps extension · 12 · RIR 3 · light band
  6. 2 × Negative dip · max · RIR 2  (rest 60s)

## Session D — Lower body B  [d-lower-b, ~42 min]
Warm-up:
  1. Bodyweight squat · 20
  2. Walking lunge · 10/side · bodyweight
  3. Glute bridge · 15
Main:
  1. 3 × Single-leg Romanian deadlift · 8/side · RIR 3 · bodyweight  (rest 90s)
  2. 2 × Walking lunge · 12/side · RIR 3 · bodyweight  (rest 75s)
  3. 2 × Single-leg glute bridge · 12-15/side · RIR 3  (rest 75s)
  4. 2 × Sliding leg curl · 10-12 · RIR 3  (rest 60s)
  5. superset × 4 rounds (rest 60s after each round)
       - Side plank · 30s/side
       - Russian twist · 15/side · RIR 3 · bodyweight
  6. 2 × Single-leg calf raise · 15-20/side · RIR 3 · bodyweight  (rest 45s)
