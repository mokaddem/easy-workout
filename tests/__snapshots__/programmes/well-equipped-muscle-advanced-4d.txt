# 8-week muscle gain — 4 sessions/week, 8 weeks
Profile: muscle, advanced, 4 days, 75 min, 85 kg
Progression: +2.5 kg upper / +5 kg lower

Phases:
  W1-2 Re-adaptation: RIR 3 (all-sets)
  W3-4 Volume: +1 set on main blocks 1, 2
  W5-6 Intensity: RIR 1 (first-set)
  W7 Peak: RIR 1 (last-set)
  W8 Deload + test: volume × 0.6; RIR 3 (all-sets); max test: Push-up

## Session A — Upper body A  [a-upper-a, ~57 min]
Warm-up:
  1. Arm circles · 30s
  2. Push-up · 15 · tempo 2-0-2 · bodyweight
  3. 2 × Band pull-apart · 20 · extra-light band  (rest 30s)
Main:
  1. 5 × Dumbbell bench press · 6-10 · RIR 2 · 17.5 kg  (rest 120s)
  2. 5 × One-arm row · 10/side · RIR 2 · 25 kg  (rest 105s)
  3. 3 × Standing shoulder press · 10-12 · RIR 2 · 15 kg  (rest 75s)
  4. 5 × Chin-up · 6-10 · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 105s)
  5. superset × 4 rounds (rest 60s after each round)
       - Dumbbell lateral raise · 15 · RIR 2 · 7.5 kg
       - Dumbbell biceps curl · 10-12 · RIR 2 · 15 kg
  6. superset × 4 rounds (rest 60s after each round)
       - Overhead triceps extension · 12 · RIR 2 · 15 kg
       - Band face pull · 15-20 · RIR 2 · medium band

## Session B — Lower body A  [b-lower-a, ~54 min]
Warm-up:
  1. Bodyweight squat · 20
  2. Walking lunge · 10/side · bodyweight
  3. Glute bridge · 15
Main:
  1. 5 × Goblet squat · 6-12 · RIR 2 · 25 kg  (rest 120s)
  2. 4 × Romanian deadlift · 10 · RIR 2 · tempo 3-0-2 · 30 kg  (rest 105s)
  3. 4 × Bulgarian split squat · 10/side · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 90s)
  4. 3 × Single-leg calf raise · 15-20/side · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 45s)
  5. superset × 5 rounds (rest 60s after each round)
       - Towel body saw · 10-12 · RIR 2
       - Toes to bar · 10-12 · RIR 2
  6. 3 × Band hip abduction · 20/side · RIR 2 · medium band  (rest 45s)

## Session C — Upper body B  [c-upper-b, ~52 min]
Warm-up:
  1. Arm circles · 30s
  2. 2 × Dead hang · 20s  (rest 30s)
  3. 2 × Band face pull · 20 · extra-light band  (rest 30s)
Main:
  1. 5 × Pull-up · 5-8 · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 150s)
  2. 5 × Standing shoulder press · 6-10 · RIR 2 · 15 kg  (rest 120s)
  3. 3 × Bent-over row · 12 · RIR 2 · 17.5 kg  (rest 75s)
  4. 5 × Push-up · 8-12 · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 90s)
  5. superset × 5 rounds (rest 60s after each round)
       - Hammer curl · 12 · RIR 2 · 15 kg
       - Overhead triceps extension · 12 · RIR 2 · 15 kg
  6. 2 × Dip · max · RIR 1 · bodyweight  (rest 60s)

## Session D — Lower body B  [d-lower-b, ~53 min]
Warm-up:
  1. Bodyweight squat · 20
  2. Walking lunge · 10/side · bodyweight
  3. Glute bridge · 15
  4. 1 × Deadlift · 10 · 15 kg  (rest 30s)
  5. 1 × Deadlift · 5 · 22.5 kg  (rest 60s)
Main:
  1. 5 × Deadlift · 6-8 · RIR 2 · 30 kg  (rest 150s)
  2. 3 × Walking lunge · 12/side · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 75s)
  3. 3 × Bench hip thrust · 12-15 · RIR 2 · 32.5 kg  (rest 75s)
  4. 3 × Suspension hamstring curl · 10-12 · RIR 2  (rest 60s)
  5. superset × 5 rounds (rest 60s after each round)

       - Russian twist · 15/side · RIR 2 · bodyweight
  6. 3 × Single-leg calf raise · 15-20/side · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 45s)
