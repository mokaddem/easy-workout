# 8-week muscle gain — 5 sessions/week, 8 weeks
Profile: muscle, intermediate, 5 days, 60 min, 75 kg
Progression: +4 kg upper / +4 kg lower

Phases:
  W1-2 Re-adaptation: RIR 3 (all-sets)
  W3-4 Volume: +1 set on main blocks 1, 2
  W5-6 Intensity: RIR 1 (first-set)
  W7 Peak: RIR 1 (last-set)
  W8 Deload + test: volume × 0.6; RIR 3 (all-sets); max test: Push-up

## Session A — Push  [a-push, ~46 min]
Warm-up:
  1. Arm circles · 30s
  2. Push-up · 15 · tempo 2-0-2 · bodyweight
  3. 2 × Prone Y raise · 20  (rest 30s)
Main:
  1. 4 × Dumbbell bench press · 6-10 · RIR 2 · 12 kg  (rest 120s)
  2. 3 × Push-up · 8-12 · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 90s)
  3. 3 × Standing shoulder press · 10-12 · RIR 2 · 8 kg  (rest 75s)
  4. 3 × Dumbbell floor fly · 12-15 · RIR 2 · 4 kg  (rest 60s)
  5. superset × 4 rounds (rest 60s after each round)
       - Dumbbell lateral raise · 15 · RIR 2 · 4 kg
       - Overhead triceps extension · 12 · RIR 2 · 8 kg
  6. 2 × Dip · max · RIR 1 · bodyweight  (rest 60s)
  7. 3 × Dumbbell reverse fly · 15-20 · RIR 2 · 4 kg  (rest 45s)

## Session B — Pull  [b-pull, ~51 min]
Warm-up:
  1. 2 × Quadruped thoracic rotation · 10/side  (rest 30s)
  2. 2 × Prone Y raise · 20  (rest 30s)
Main:
  1. 4 × Bent-over row · 6-12 · RIR 2 · 8 kg  (rest 90s)
  2. 4 × One-arm row · 10/side · RIR 2 · 12 kg  (rest 105s)
  3. 3 × Inverted row under a table · 12-15 · RIR 2  (rest 75s)
  4. 3 × Renegade row · 12/side · RIR 2 · 8 kg  (rest 75s)
  5. superset × 3 rounds (rest 60s after each round)
       - Dumbbell biceps curl · 10-12 · RIR 2 · 8 kg
       - Hammer curl · 12 · RIR 2 · 8 kg
  6. 3 × Shrug · 15 · RIR 2 · 12 kg  (rest 45s)
  7. 3 × Dumbbell reverse fly · 15-20 · RIR 2 · 4 kg  (rest 45s)

## Session C — Lower body + core  [c-lower-core, ~59 min]
Warm-up:
  1. Bodyweight squat · 20
  2. Walking lunge · 10/side · bodyweight
  3. Glute bridge · 15
Main:
  1. 4 × Goblet squat · 8-12 · RIR 2 · 12 kg  (rest 120s)
  2. 3 × Bulgarian split squat · 10/side · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 90s)
  3. 4 × Romanian deadlift · 10 · RIR 2 · tempo 3-0-2 · 12 kg  (rest 105s)
  4. 3 × Step-up · 12/side · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 60s)
  5. 3 × Single-leg calf raise · 15-20/side · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 45s)
  6. superset × 3 rounds (rest 60s after each round)
       - Plank · 45-60s
       - Reverse crunch · 10-12 · RIR 2
  7. 3 × Russian twist · 15/side · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 45s)

## Session D — Upper-body density  [d-upper-density, ~45 min]
Warm-up:
  1. Arm circles · 30s
  2. 2 × Prone Y raise · 20  (rest 30s)
  3. Quadruped thoracic rotation · 10/side
Main:
  1. circuit × 4 rounds (rest 75s after each round)
       - Wide push-up · 12-15 · RIR 2
       - Bent-over row · 12 · RIR 2 · 8 kg
       - Seated shoulder press · 12 · RIR 2 · 8 kg
  2. circuit × 4 rounds (rest 75s after each round)
       - Renegade row · 8-12/side · RIR 1 · 8 kg
       - Close-grip push-up · 10-12 · RIR 2
       - Dumbbell lateral raise · 20 · RIR 2 · 4 kg
  3. circuit × 3 rounds (rest 75s after each round)
       - Dumbbell 21s · 21 · RIR 2 · 8 kg
       - Dumbbell triceps kickback · 15/side · RIR 2 · 4 kg
       - Dumbbell reverse fly · 20 · RIR 2 · 4 kg

## Session E — Lower body + metabolic full body  [e-lower-metabolic, ~55 min]
Warm-up:
  1. Bodyweight squat · 20
  2. Walking lunge · 10/side · bodyweight
  3. Glute bridge · 15
  4. 1 × Deadlift · 10 · 4 kg  (rest 30s)
  5. 1 × Deadlift · 5 · 8 kg  (rest 60s)
Main:
  1. 4 × Deadlift · 6-8 · RIR 2 · 12 kg  (rest 150s)
  2. 3 × Goblet sumo squat · 12-15 · RIR 2 · 12 kg  (rest 105s)
  3. 3 × Bench hip thrust · 15 · RIR 2 · 12 kg  (rest 75s)
  4. 3 × Walking lunge · 12/side · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 75s)
  5. 3 × Side-lying leg raise · 20/side · RIR 2  (rest 45s)
  6. 3 × Sliding leg curl · 10-12 · RIR 2  (rest 60s)
  7. 3 × Single-leg calf raise · 15-20/side · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 45s)
