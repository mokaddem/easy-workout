# 8-week recomposition — 6 sessions/week, 8 weeks
Profile: recomposition, intermediate, 6 days, 45 min
Progression: +2 kg upper / +4 kg lower

Phases:
  W1-2 Re-adaptation: RIR 3 (all-sets)
  W3-4 Volume: +1 set on main blocks 1, 2; cardio +5 min
  W5-6 Intensity: RIR 1 (first-set); cardio +10 min
  W7 Peak: RIR 1 (last-set); cardio +10 min
  W8 Deload + test: volume × 0.6; RIR 3 (all-sets); max test: Push-up

## Session A — Push  [a-push, ~48 min]
Warm-up:
  1. Arm circles · 30s
  2. Push-up · 15 · tempo 2-0-2 · bodyweight
  3. 2 × Band pull-apart · 20 · light band  (rest 30s)
Main:
  1. 4 × Floor press · 8-10 · RIR 2 · 10 kg  (rest 90s)
  2. 3 × Push-up · 8-12 · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 90s)
  3. 3 × Standing shoulder press · 10-12 · RIR 2 · 10 kg  (rest 75s)
  4. 3 × Dumbbell floor fly · 12-15 · RIR 2 · 4 kg  (rest 60s)
  5. superset × 3 rounds (rest 60s after each round)
       - Dumbbell lateral raise · 15 · RIR 2 · 4 kg
       - Overhead triceps extension · 12 · RIR 2 · 8 kg
  6. 2 × Dip · max · RIR 1 · bodyweight  (rest 60s)
Finisher:
  1. Treadmill incline walk · 15 min @ 5 km/h, 12%

## Session B — Pull  [b-pull, ~49 min]
Warm-up:
  1. 2 × Dead hang · 20s  (rest 30s)
  2. 2 × Band face pull · 20 · light band  (rest 30s)
Main:
  1. 4 × Pull-up · 5-8 · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 120s)
  2. 4 × One-arm row · 10/side · RIR 2 · 14 kg  (rest 90s)
  3. 3 × Horizontal band row · 12-15 · RIR 2 · medium band  (rest 75s)
  4. 3 × Bent-over row · 12 · RIR 2 · 10 kg  (rest 75s)
  5. superset × 3 rounds (rest 60s after each round)
       - Dumbbell biceps curl · 10-12 · RIR 2 · 8 kg
       - Hammer curl · 12 · RIR 2 · 8 kg
  6. 3 × Shrug · 15 · RIR 2 · 20 kg  (rest 45s)
Finisher:
  1. Treadmill incline walk · 10 min @ 4.5 km/h, 15%

## Session C — Lower body + core  [c-lower-core, ~49 min]
Warm-up:
  1. Bodyweight squat · 20
  2. Walking lunge · 10/side · bodyweight
  3. Glute bridge · 15
Main:
  1. 4 × Goblet squat · 10-12 · RIR 2 · 14 kg  (rest 90s)
  2. 3 × Bulgarian split squat · 10/side · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 90s)
  3. 4 × Romanian deadlift · 10 · RIR 2 · tempo 3-0-2 · 18 kg  (rest 90s)
  4. 3 × Step-up · 12/side · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 60s)
  5. 3 × Single-leg calf raise · 15-20/side · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 45s)
  6. superset × 3 rounds (rest 60s after each round)
       - Plank · 45-60s
       - Hanging leg raise · 10-12 · RIR 2
  7. 3 × Russian twist · 15/side · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 45s)

## Active recovery — incline walk  [cardio, ~40 min]
Main:
  1. Treadmill incline walk · 35-40 min @ 5.5 km/h, 10%

## Session D — Upper-body density  [d-upper-density, ~42 min]
Warm-up:
  1. Arm circles · 30s
  2. 2 × Band pull-apart · 20 · light band  (rest 30s)
  3. Dead hang · 20s
Main:
  1. circuit × 3 rounds (rest 45s after each round)
       - Wide push-up · 12-15 · RIR 2
       - Bent-over row · 12 · RIR 2 · 10 kg
       - Seated shoulder press · 12 · RIR 2 · 10 kg
  2. circuit × 3 rounds (rest 45s after each round)
       - Chin-up · max · RIR 1 · bodyweight
       - Close-grip push-up · 10-12 · RIR 2
       - Band lateral raise · 20 · RIR 2 · light band
  3. circuit × 3 rounds (rest 45s after each round)
       - Dumbbell 21s · 21 · RIR 2 · 8 kg
       - Dumbbell triceps kickback · 15/side · RIR 2 · 4 kg
       - Band face pull · 20 · RIR 2 · light band
Finisher:
  1. Treadmill incline walk · 10 min

## Session E — Lower body + metabolic full body  [e-lower-metabolic, ~47 min]
Warm-up:
  1. Bodyweight squat · 20
  2. Walking lunge · 10/side · bodyweight
  3. Glute bridge · 15
  4. 1 × Deadlift · 10 · 8 kg  (rest 30s)
  5. 1 × Deadlift · 5 · 12 kg  (rest 60s)
Main:
  1. 4 × Deadlift · 8 · RIR 2 · 18 kg  (rest 120s)
  2. 3 × Goblet sumo squat · 12-15 · RIR 2 · 14 kg  (rest 90s)
  3. 3 × Floor hip thrust · 15 · RIR 2 · 18 kg  (rest 75s)
  4. 3 × Walking lunge · 12/side · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 75s)
Finisher:
  1. circuit × 4 rounds (rest 60s after each round)
       - Dumbbell swing · 15 · 14 kg
       - Mountain climber · 30
       - Jump squat · 15
       - Side plank · 30s/side
