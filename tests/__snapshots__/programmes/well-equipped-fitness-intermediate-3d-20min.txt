# 8-week general fitness — 3 sessions/week, 8 weeks
Profile: general-fitness, intermediate, 3 days, 20 min
Progression: +2.5 kg upper / +5 kg lower

Phases:
  W1-3 Re-adaptation: RIR 3 (all-sets)
  W4-5 Volume: +1 set on main blocks 1, 2; cardio +5 min
  W6-7 Intensity: RIR 2 (first-set); cardio +10 min
  W8 Deload + test: volume × 0.6; RIR 3 (all-sets); max test: Push-up

## Session A — Full body A  [a-full-a, ~22 min]
Warm-up:
  1. Arm circles · 30s
  2. Bodyweight squat · 15
Main:
  1. 2 × Goblet squat · 10-15 · RIR 2 · 15 kg  (rest 60s)
  2. 2 × Dumbbell bench press · 10-12 · RIR 2 · 10 kg  (rest 60s)
  3. 2 × One-arm row · 12/side · RIR 2 · 15 kg  (rest 30s)
  4. 2 × Romanian deadlift · 12 · RIR 2 · tempo 3-0-2 · 17.5 kg  (rest 30s)
  5. superset × 2 rounds (rest 30s after each round)
       - Plank · 45-60s
       - Hanging leg raise · 10-12 · RIR 2
Finisher:
  1. Rowing machine · 5 min

## Session B — Full body B  [b-full-b, ~21 min]
Warm-up:
  1. Arm circles · 30s
  2. Bodyweight squat · 15
  3. 1 × Band pull-apart · 15 · extra-light band  (rest 30s)
  4. 1 × Deadlift · 10 · 7.5 kg  (rest 30s)
  5. 1 × Deadlift · 5 · 12.5 kg  (rest 60s)
Main:
  1. 3 × Deadlift · 10 · RIR 2 · 17.5 kg  (rest 60s)
  2. 3 × Pull-up · 6-10 · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 60s)
  3. 2 × Bulgarian split squat · 12/side · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 30s)
  4. 2 × Standing shoulder press · 10-12 · RIR 2 · 10 kg  (rest 60s)
Finisher:
  1. Rowing machine · 5 min

## Session C — Full body C  [c-full-c, ~22 min]
Warm-up:
  1. Arm circles · 30s
  2. Bodyweight squat · 15
  3. 2 × Band pull-apart · 15 · extra-light band  (rest 30s)
Main:
  1. 3 × Front squat · 12-15 · RIR 2 · 10 kg  (rest 60s)
  2. 3 × Bent-over row · 15 · RIR 2 · 10 kg  (rest 45s)
  3. 2 × Dumbbell bench press · 12-15 · RIR 2 · 10 kg  (rest 60s)
  4. 2 × Walking lunge · 15/side · RIR 2 · bodyweight  (rest 45s)
  5. superset × 2 rounds (rest 30s after each round)
       - Plank · 45-60s
       - Superman · 12 · RIR 2
